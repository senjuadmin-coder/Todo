## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, calm, intentional, and expensive aesthetic tailored for **MOMENTUM**, a personal productivity web application. Designed for thoughtful professionals, writers, and creatives, the interface evokes the quiet focus of a sunlit architectural studio or a finely bound linen notebook.

The visual language blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Architectural Precision**:
- **Editorial Minimalism:** Generous whitespace, unhurried typographic hierarchy, and a restrained, earthy color palette that eliminates visual noise.
- **Architectural Precision:** Crisp 1px structural borders, deliberate grid alignment, and tactile, paper-like tonal shifts replace generic SaaS drop shadows, neon gradients, and glassmorphism.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in warm, natural pigments that reduce eye strain during extended focus sessions and create a tactile, paper-like atmosphere.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - **Primary Background (`#F4F1EA`):** Warm ivory serves as the foundational canvas, providing a soft, sunlit backdrop.
  - **Secondary Surface (`#E8E4DA`):** Soft beige is used for sidebars, recessed panels, hover states, and secondary containers.
- **Ink & Borders:**
  - **Primary Text (`#20211F`):** Almost-black ink ensures crisp legibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
  - **Hairline Borders (`#D8D3C8`):** Subtle warm grey defines structural 1px grid lines, dividers, and container edges.
- **Accents:**
  - **Primary Accent (`#D97745`):** Burnt orange is reserved for high-priority actions, active states, and focal indicators. Use sparingly to preserve its impact.
  - **Secondary Accent (`#89917A`):** Muted olive supports secondary statuses, completion states, and calm organizational tags.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs **Newsreader**, a high-contrast editorial serif, with **Inter**, a clean, utilitarian sans-serif.

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`):** Oversized page titles, daily date headers, and section headings use Newsreader with tight negative letter-spacing and generous line-height. Avoid bold weights for display sizes; rely on scale and contrast rather than heavy strokes.
- **Functional UI & Body (`Inter`):** Task descriptions, metadata, timestamps, and interactive controls use Inter for effortless scanning at compact sizes.
- **Responsive Scaling:** Display and large headline levels step down on mobile viewports (`display-lg-mobile`, `headline-lg-mobile`) to preserve breathing room without crowding the screen.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **12-column fluid grid** with generous outer margins and disciplined internal spacing to create an architectural, uncrowded workspace.
